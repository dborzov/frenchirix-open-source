      This help

      Underlined pieces of an annotated line are its units: blue for a word, grey for an expression, dotted when not aligned yet; a small ? marks timing worth checking by ear. ≠ beside a line: the spoken French differs from the subtitle, click to compare. A small number after a phrase links to its note for a child (reference or wordplay); ※ in the list marks scenes that have notes.

      A review mark says that a line, or one of its units, needs fixing: its category and your comment go to the agent that fixes it. ⚑ in the gutter marks a line (click it to add one); marks save to the database at once; an open mark can be edited or deleted until it is resolved.
